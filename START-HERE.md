# Start here

## The working loop

1. **Capture** a paper or thought in `00-inbox/`.
2. **Triage** papers into the initial must-read pool or a topic pool.
3. **Read** with a specific question in mind and create a paper note.
4. **Connect** the paper to a topic, idea, tension, or open question.
5. **Synthesize** once a week rather than waiting until the end.
6. **Evaluate** emerging topics using evidence and personal interest.

## First phase: landscape orientation

For the first few weeks, the goal is breadth with enough depth to recognize:

- the major AI-agent paradigms and vocabulary;
- influential systems and foundational papers;
- common evaluation methods and benchmarks;
- recurring limitations, disagreements, and open problems;
- topics that sustain your personal curiosity.

The initial pool is intentionally empty. We will choose it next rather than hard-code a reading list before agreeing on scope.

## A practical reading cadence

- **Quick scan (10–20 minutes):** decide whether a paper is relevant and why.
- **Focused read (45–90 minutes):** extract the contribution, evidence, limitations, and connections.
- **Deep read:** reserve for foundational papers and work central to an emerging topic.
- **Weekly synthesis (30–60 minutes):** update the landscape map and topic candidates.

## File naming

- Paper notes: `YYYY-first-author-short-title.md`
- Topic folders: short lowercase names with hyphens, such as `agent-evaluation`
- Idea cards: `YYYY-MM-DD-short-name.md`
- Weekly reviews: `YYYY-MM-DD-weekly-synthesis.md`

## Status vocabulary

Use these consistently in paper pools and notes:

`candidate` → `queued` → `reading` → `read` → `synthesized`

Use `parked` when something is intentionally set aside and `dropped` when it is no longer useful.

## Definition of “done” for a paper

A paper is not fully processed until:

- its paper note captures the main claim and evidence;
- limitations or uncertainties are recorded;
- it is linked to at least one topic, idea, or landscape theme;
- the pool entry has been updated to `synthesized`.

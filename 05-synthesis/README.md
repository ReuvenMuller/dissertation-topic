# Synthesis

Synthesis turns a collection of notes into a view of the field.

- Update [landscape-map.md](landscape-map.md) as major areas and relationships become clearer.
- Create one weekly review from [templates/weekly-synthesis.md](../templates/weekly-synthesis.md).
- Write claims in your own words and link them back to paper notes.
- Distinguish what the literature demonstrates from what you infer.

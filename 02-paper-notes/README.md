# Paper notes

Create one note per paper by copying [templates/paper-note.md](../templates/paper-note.md).

Paper notes should be selective, analytical, and written in your own words. The most useful sections are usually:

- the paper's actual claim;
- what evidence supports it;
- what remains uncertain;
- how it changes your map of the field;
- what research questions it creates.

For citation accuracy, always verify bibliographic details against the paper or publisher page.

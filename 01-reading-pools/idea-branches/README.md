# Idea-branch paper pools

These pools are deliberately separate from the initial landscape and topic queues. Each one tests a specific research idea without prematurely declaring it a dissertation topic.

## Active branches

- [Agent organization and work procedures](agent-organization-and-procedures/README.md) — ten-paper exploration of organizational foundations, work procedures, failure analysis, and synergy, with discussion notes and explicit novelty cautions.
- [Cost-aware heterogeneous agent harnesses](heterogeneous-agent-harnesses/README.md) — assigning different models to architect, planner, executor, verifier, and related roles while measuring quality, reliability, cost, and latency.

A paper may be referenced in several pools, but it should have only one row in `../paper-inventory.csv` and one eventual paper note.

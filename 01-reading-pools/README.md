# Reading pools

Reading pools answer two different questions:

- `initial-must-read/`: What gives me a credible orientation to the field?
- `topics/`: What do I need to understand a specific possible dissertation topic?
- `idea-branches/`: What do I need to test a specific idea before promoting it to a candidate topic?
- `industry-reports/`: What do production systems, engineering experiments, architecture guides, and practitioner surveys claim happens outside academic benchmarks?

The canonical inventory is [paper-inventory.csv](paper-inventory.csv). The Markdown pool files are the human-readable working views and may include richer reasoning.

Industry material is deliberately separate. Its canonical inventory is [industry-reports/industry-inventory.csv](industry-reports/industry-inventory.csv); entries there are not papers and should not be counted as peer-reviewed evidence.

## Pool discipline

- Record why a paper is in a pool before adding it.
- Keep each topic's active queue small enough to remain intentional.
- A paper may appear in multiple pools but should have only one paper note.
- Prefer durable paper or publisher links over copied PDFs in Git.
- Mark surveys and position papers clearly; do not treat them as equivalent to empirical evidence.
